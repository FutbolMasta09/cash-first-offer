# Target Audience — Cash-first offer

Authored from `docs/OFFER_COPY.md` "who this is for" / "who this is not for" — one paragraph, per runbook Phase 1 requirement.

---

## Primary Audience

**Who:** Owners of small service businesses (1–10 people) — agencies, restaurants, trades, coaches, shops.
**What they need:** They waste hours on repetitive writing, intake, training, or content calendars, and are "busy, not AI curious" — they want something they click and use, not a strategy PDF or a course.
**Where they are:** Fiverr, Upwork, small-business Reddit/Discord communities, referrals.
**What they pay:** Used to paying fixed prices for scoped deliverables, not subscriptions or open-ended retainers. Price-sensitive enough that packages are priced at $497 / $1,200 flat, not hourly.

## Audience Implications

| Decision area | Implication for this audience |
|---|---|
| UI complexity | Simple — one-page landing, two CTAs, no login |
| Onboarding | Minimal — pay, then a 24-hour brief request, then delivery |
| Content tone | Direct, practical-operator voice; no agency gloss, no fake testimonials |
| Session length | Minutes (landing page); days (delivery cycle) |
| Platform | Mobile-readable web page first |
| Price sensitivity | Medium — fixed packages beat hourly, but priced above "bottom-feeder" gig pricing |

---

## What This Audience Is NOT

- Not people who want a free consultation or discovery call before paying
- Not people who want a full brochure-website rebuild
- Not people who want "build my SaaS idea" or ongoing feature work
- Not enterprise buyers or teams needing procurement/compliance review
