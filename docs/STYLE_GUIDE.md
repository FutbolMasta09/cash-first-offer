# Style Guide — Cash-first offer

Thin, per D001 (service-experiment profile) — locked before the landing page is built. Not a full game/app style guide; see `docs/OFFER_COPY.md` for the actual paste-ready copy.

---

## Tone & Voice

**Overall tone:** Direct, practical operator — someone who ships, not an agency pitching a vision.
**Vocabulary level:** Casual adult, plain English. No jargon, no "AI transformation" buzzwords.
**Things to avoid:** Fake testimonials, hype ("revolutionary," "game-changing"), vague scope ("AI consulting"), purple-glow/generic-AI visual clichés (gradient blobs, robot hands, neon circuit patterns).

**Sample approved sentence:**
> I install a working AI + web workflow in your small business in 7 days — a tool you can actually use, not a strategy PDF.

**Sample rejected sentence:**
> Unlock the transformative power of AI to revolutionize your business operations today!

---

## Visual Identity

Keep it plain and credible, not agency-glossy:

- **Palette:** Neutral background, one accent color, high contrast text. No default AI-generated purple/blue gradients.
- **Typography:** System font stack or one simple web font (e.g. Inter). No decorative display fonts.
- **Imagery:** None required for v1 — text-first landing page. If used, real/plain screenshots over stock photography or AI-generated hero art.
- **Minimum tap target:** 44×44px on mobile CTAs.
- **Minimum font size:** 16px body text.

---

## Naming Conventions

**Project/brand name:** "Cash-first offer" internally; the public-facing name is whatever headline/package language is in `docs/OFFER_COPY.md` (no separate product brand needed yet).
**How to refer to it in copy:** Describe by outcome ("AI workflow install," "AI operating kit," "custom mini-tool"), not by an invented brand name.
**Package names:** "AI Operating Kit" (Package A), "Custom Mini-Tool" (Package B) — use exactly as written in `docs/OFFER_COPY.md` everywhere (landing, Fiverr, Upwork).

---

## Asset Rules

- No fake testimonials or manufactured social proof — see `docs/HANDOFF_LANDING_PAGE.md` acceptance criteria
- No AI-generated stock imagery presented as real photos/clients
- Same offer copy everywhere — do not draft new custom copy per platform
