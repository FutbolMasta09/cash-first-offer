## Why not Fiverr/Upwork first?

| Factor | Gig marketplaces | This offer ($497 / $1,200) |
|--------|------------------|----------------------------|
| Buyer mindset | Price-shop, template gigs | Fixed scope, working install |
| Typical AI gigs | $25–$150 | $497 minimum |
| Who buys | Strangers optimizing for cheap | Small service biz owners via trust |
| Ryan's edge | Weak — competes on price | Speed, fixed package, Ryan builds |

Small business owners drowning in intake/content work usually find help via **referrals, Facebook groups, local networks, and creators they follow** — not Fiverr browse.

**Rule:** Do not lower price to fit marketplaces. Fiverr/Upwork are **optional Day 10+** only.

## Tier 2 — fill toward 10–20 (Days 1–14)

| Channel | Target touches | Action |
|---------|----------------|--------|

**SWC guardrails** ([`FTF_CATALOG.md`](FTF_CATALOG.md)): frame as Ryan taking client installs; fixed packages + landing; one post or few DMs max.
