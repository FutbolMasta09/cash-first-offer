## Package A — AI Operating Kit ($497)

### Simple explanation (elevator version)

**What it is:** Ryan sets up a creator or small business so they stop reinventing ChatGPT every week. They get a **custom prompt library**, a **one-page “how we use AI” guide**, and a **personalized GPT or Claude Project** that sounds like their business — delivered over a weekend.

**Who it’s for:** Creators, coaches, small service businesses, or anyone who already uses AI but it’s messy, inconsistent, or only in their head.

**Who it’s NOT for:** People who want a full website, a mobile app, or “build my SaaS idea.”

**One sentence you could say on camera:**  
*“If you’re still copying random ChatGPT prompts, Ryan does a $497 weekend install — your prompts, your rules, your voice, done.”*

---

### Detailed explanation (full scope)

**Official name:** Package A — AI Operating Kit  
**Price:** $497 — paid in full upfront  
**Turnaround:** Weekend after payment clears  
**Tagline:** Your AI playbook installed this weekend.

**What the client gets:**

1. **Prompt library tailored to their business**  
   Examples of what gets covered (client picks what applies): product/listing descriptions, client emails, social captions, follow-ups, intake replies — organized so their team can reuse them instead of starting from scratch every time.

2. **One-page SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)**  
   A single page that explains *how their team uses AI without chaos* — when to use which prompts, what not to paste into public tools, basic quality checks. Not a 50-page manual; one page people will actually read.

3. **One custom GPT or Claude Project**  
   Configured to their voice, rules, and business context so day-to-day work happens inside a dedicated assistant — not generic ChatGPT with no memory of how they operate.

4. **30-minute handoff call**  
   Walkthrough of what was built and how to use it. Async Loom video is fine if schedules don’t align.

**What is NOT included:**

- Custom app or website build  
- Hosting or ongoing maintenance  
- Ongoing support retainer  
- Ryan paying for the client’s API keys or tool subscriptions  

**Payment:** Full $497 upfront via Ryan’s payment link (he sends in DM — never in public posts).

**Best audience on Following the Ferrells:**  
Use **creator-flavored** language — fellow creators, UGC people, small digital businesses, “laptop on the road” energy. Generic small-business wording is fine on the landing page; your posts can sound like *us*, not a Fiverr gig.
