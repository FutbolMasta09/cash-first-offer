## 5. Social media timing — the most common mistake

**Start during development, not at launch.**

The community that buys on day 1 is the one that watched the project get built. Starting social media the week you launch means talking to nobody. Starting during development — even casually, once a week — compounds.

**Rule:**
- As soon as active development begins, start a weekly dev-progress post
- Format: one screenshot or GIF + one sentence on what shipped that week
- Doesn't have to be polished — authentic > produced
- No dedicated account required — personal voice from personal account works

## 9. What NOT to do

- **Don't spam.** One thoughtful post per community beats five low-effort ones.
- **Don't oversell.** Be honest about current state and what's coming.
- **Don't pay for ads before product-market fit.** Organic first. Paid acquisition makes sense after you have signals.
- **Don't start a dedicated social account at zero and abandon it.** An inactive account is a red flag. Only start it if you'll post to it.
- **Don't ignore comments.** Every comment in the first 30 days is data. Respond to everything.
